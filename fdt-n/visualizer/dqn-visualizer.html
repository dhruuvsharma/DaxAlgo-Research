FDT-N + DQN Trading Agent

Interactive visualizer · deep Q-learning policy on frozen transformer forecasts
Research artifact. A reinforcement-learning demo: a small double-DQN learns a position policy on top of the frozen FDT-N transformer's forecasts. Simulated fills with a fixed spread — not a trading system, not investment advice.

What is this?

The FDT-N transformer (371,861 parameters, trained on EURUSD footprint bars, frozen) predicts a distribution over the next 8 minutes of market structure: POC, low, high, buy/sell POC, relative volume, and delta fraction. This demo attaches a Deep Q-Network (111,619 parameters, 3-layer MLP) that reads those 168 forecasts each minute and chooses a position: short, flat, or long. The agent was trained with epsilon-greedy exploration and experience replay on 19 trading days, tuned on 3 validation days, and evaluated once, greedily, on the final 3 unseen days.

Reward design

Each minute the agent earns r = position × Δpoc − cost where Δpoc is the normalized one-bar POC change and cost is the 0.2-pip spread charged only when the position changes. γ=0.995 makes the agent care about roughly the next few minutes of path — matching the transformer's 8-bar forecast horizon. Episodes are single trading days; weekends and day boundaries end an episode (no overnight positions).

Checkpoint selection

Every 10 epochs the greedy policy was run on 3 validation days. The best checkpoint (epoch ) was kept; training continued to 160 but later epochs drifted lower on validation, so the early-epoch policy was used for the test evaluation.

What to look for

  • Train equity climbs as ε falls — the agent exploits what it learned
  • Validation equity is noisy day-to-day (only 3 days) — do not over-read small moves
  • The ε curve decays exponentially; from epoch ~70 the agent is mostly greedy
