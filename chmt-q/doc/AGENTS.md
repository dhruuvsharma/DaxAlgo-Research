  Q-learning.
- The implemented model has 2,089,554 trainable parameters. Earlier material containing 2,089,518
  is a superseded design ledger.
- Better aggregate pinball loss did not establish useful conditional medians, executable edge, or
  profitability. Keep the current model research-only and non-promotable.
- June 25–30 is process-accessed and retired from confirmatory use. Do not call it sealed,
  untouched, or an outer-holdout result.
- The Transformer–Double-DQN material is proposed, unimplemented, untrained, and unvalidated.
- Do not commit raw exchange data, prediction tapes, model weights, checkpoints, credentials, or
  licensed material without an explicit release decision.
